## AI / ML / MLOps Integration Layer

- AI/ML topic: AI product case study: user problem, MVP scope, eval results, cost/latency tradeoffs, and failure analysis.
- MLOps topic: Readiness review: what is monitored, what can rollback, what needs human review, what remains manual.
- LLM topic: Explain model/provider choice and why the product does or does not need a more advanced model.
- Product/communication topic: User interview notes, PRD, MVP spec, pitch outline, stakeholder memo, and roadmap tradeoffs.
- Resource mapping: primary: [YC Startup School](https://www.startupschool.org/), [Google Project Management Certificate](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/google-project-management), [Google Technical Writing](https://developers.google.com/tech-writing). Optional: [Startup School Library](https://www.ycombinator.com/library) for customer discovery and MVP examples.
- Required artifact: AI product case study with user interviews, PRD, MVP spec, pitch outline, technical writing samples, and evidence links.
- Interview explanation: Explain the user problem, product metric, technical tradeoff, and how you would prioritize next.
- What not to study this week: Pitch theater, market-size rabbit holes, and business docs not tied to shipped artifacts.
